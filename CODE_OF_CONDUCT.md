# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

Expected behavior includes:

- being considerate and constructive;
- discussing ideas without attacking people;
- accepting feedback and correcting mistakes;
- respecting privacy and avoiding publication of sensitive data;
- stepping in or asking maintainers for help when a discussion becomes unsafe.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing credentials or private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or edit contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Reports should be made privately through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) when they contain sensitive information; otherwise use a GitHub issue or discussion with only the necessary context.

This code applies to project spaces and to public interactions when someone is representing the project.
